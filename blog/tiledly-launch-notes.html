Published October 11, 2026 · By the Tiledly Team
Official launch announcement — what we shipped, what we learned, and where we're going

We're Live

Tiledly v1.0 launched publicly on October 1, 2026, and the response has exceeded every expectation we set for ourselves in early development. As of today, more than 1,200 players have completed at least one daily challenge in the first week — a number that would have felt wildly optimistic just six months ago when this project was still a prototype running on a local server.

This post is our formal launch note: a record of what shipped in v1.0, how the core systems work, and an honest look at the roadmap ahead. We believe in building publicly and writing down what we're doing, both for players who want to understand the game more deeply and for ourselves as a forcing function to stay accountable.

The 7 Daily Modes: A Full Breakdown

Tiledly's seven game modes cycle across the week, each targeting a distinct cognitive skill so the daily challenge stays genuinely varied rather than becoming a single-trick routine. Each mode also has four weekly variants, giving 28 distinct puzzle configurations per month before patterns begin to repeat.

Monday
🧮 Sum Hunt
Find pairs or trios of tiles that add to the day's target sum. Tests mental arithmetic and rapid pattern recognition. Variants include 2-tile, 3-tile, negative-number, and time-attack configurations.
Tuesday
🔢 Number Rush
Locate and tap numbers 1 through 25 in order across a scrambled grid. Trains peripheral scanning and visual search speed. Variants include ascending, descending, and skip-count sequences.
Wednesday
🎨 Color Cascade
Navigate color sequences and Stroop-effect interference puzzles where the color named by text and the color of the text itself conflict. One of the hardest modes to master cleanly.
Thursday
🧠 Memory Grid
Numbers are revealed for four seconds, then all tiles flip to show question marks. Tap the sequence from memory alone. Working memory capacity determines your ceiling in this mode.
Friday
💣 Minefield
Memorize hidden bomb positions before tiles disguise themselves, then clear all safe tiles in order without detonating. High penalty for mistakes makes this the most punishing mode in the rotation.
Saturday
⚡ Math Blitz
Rapid mental arithmetic under time pressure — PEMDAS equations, multiplication tables, percentage estimation, and answer classification. Variants scale difficulty from accessible to genuinely demanding.
Sunday
🔤 Alpha Hunt
Find and tap letters A through Y in sequence, or identify and spell a hidden theme word from a scrambled letter field. A satisfying end to the week for players who favor language-pattern skills.

The Global Leaderboard & Percentile Ranking

Every completed run is submitted to the global leaderboard. Your score is evaluated on three dimensions: elapsed time (millisecond precision), accuracy (mistakes incur a time penalty), and the combination of both into a final ranked score.

The percentile ranking is calculated dynamically as more players complete the day's challenge. If you finish at Top 3%, that means your score was better than 97% of all players who completed that mode on that calendar day. The percentile is recalculated continuously — finishing early in the day means your initial rank may shift as the global pool grows through the day's UTC cycle.

No account is required to appear on the leaderboard. Your identity is an anonymous persistent token stored in your browser. If you clear your storage, your history is lost — this is a deliberate trade-off that lets us avoid collecting personal data while still delivering meaningful competitive rankings.

Tiledly PRO: What It Includes and Why It Matters

Tiledly is free, and the daily challenge will stay free permanently. PRO is an optional supporter membership for players who want to go further and help fund the infrastructure and development that keeps the game running.

PRO membership includes:

  • Ad-free experience: All banner ads are removed across every game mode and result screen.
  • Full calendar archive: Replay any past daily challenge going back to launch — useful for catching up on missed days or practicing specific modes.
  • Streak protection: Miss a day? Your streak is automatically preserved once per 30-day period.
  • VIP gold crown: Your racer avatar in the Battle Royale Replay displays a distinctive gold crown, visible in any shared replay link.

PRO is billed monthly through Stripe and can be cancelled anytime via the self-service customer portal. There are no hidden fees and no annual lock-in. Every PRO subscription directly funds server costs, puzzle generation, and the development of new features described below.

Early Player Feedback & What We've Heard

The first week of feedback has been illuminating. A few consistent themes have emerged:

  • The Battle Royale Replay is the highlight. More players than we expected mentioned the replay as the moment the game "clicked" for them — when it stopped feeling like a solo experience and started feeling communal. We're doubling down on this feature.
  • Memory Grid is the hardest mode by far. A plurality of players cited Thursday as their weakest day. We're considering an optional tutorial run before the first Memory Grid challenge to help new players understand the mechanic.
  • The four-week variant cycle isn't obvious. Several players were surprised when Monday's Sum Hunt changed format in week two. We're going to add a brief mode description card before each daily challenge that explains the day's specific variant.
  • Mobile experience needs polish. The game works on mobile browsers, but several players reported tap-target sizing issues on smaller screens. This is our top UX priority for the v1.1 update.

The Roadmap: What's Coming Next

We're committing publicly to the following features in development:

  • Friends Leaderboard (Q4 2026): Create or join a private group leaderboard using a shareable invite code. Compare scores within a group of friends or colleagues without exposing your rank to the global pool.
  • Mobile App (Q1 2027): A native iOS and Android app with push notifications at the daily reset, offline puzzle caching, and haptic feedback for a more tactile tile-tapping experience.
  • New Game Mode — Pattern Trace (TBD): A spatial reasoning mode where players must reproduce a briefly-displayed tile sequence by drawing a path. Still in early prototyping — no release date yet.
  • Replay Sharing Links: Share a permalink to your Battle Royale Replay so friends can watch your run. Currently in internal testing.

None of these features will be paywalled. PRO members may receive early access to betas, but all shipped features will be available to every player. That's our commitment, and we're writing it here to hold ourselves to it.

Thank you for being here at the beginning. We're building this for you, and your scores on the leaderboard every day are proof the idea was worth pursuing.
