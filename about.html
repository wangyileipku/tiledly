About Tiledly

The Daily 60-Second Mental Sprint & Global Battle Royale

What is Tiledly?

Tiledly is a fast-paced daily mental agility challenge. Inspired by daily ritual games like Wordle and reaction speed tests, Tiledly gives players across the globe one shared puzzle every day.

Every midnight (UTC), a new challenge unlocks. All players receive the exact same seeded grid layout, meaning your time and accuracy are directly comparable with thousands of daily competitors worldwide.

The 7 Daily Modes & 4-Week Rotation Matrix

To keep your cognitive skills sharp across arithmetic, working memory, spatial orientation, and visual recognition, Tiledly cycles through 7 core game modes, featuring 4 weekly evolving variants (28 distinct challenges in total):

Monday
🧮
Sum Hunt
Find matching pairs or trios that add up to the daily target sum as fast as possible. Tests mental addition and quick pattern matching.
Tuesday
🔢
Number Rush
Sprint through numbers 1 to 25 in order (or countdown from 25 to 1). Tests peripheral vision, visual scanning, and reaction speed.
Wednesday
🎨
Color Cascade
Cycle through repeating color sequences and Stroop cognitive interference puzzles where font text conflicts with font color.
Thursday
🧠
Memory Grid
Memorize the grid in 4 seconds before all tiles flip to ?. Tap numbers in sequence purely from your working memory.
Friday
💣
Minefield
Memorize hidden bomb tiles before they disguise themselves. Clear all safe numbers in ascending order without detonating a bomb!
Saturday
⚡
Math Blitz
Rapid mental arithmetic: PEMDAS equations, multiplication tables, percentages, and target bucket classification under time pressure.
Sunday
🔤
Alpha Hunt
Locate and tap letters A through Y in sequence, or spell out hidden 5-letter theme words in record time.

How Scoring & Percentiles Work

Your performance in Tiledly is evaluated on three dimensions:

  • Elapsed Time: Measured with millisecond precision using the browser's high-resolution performance timer.
  • Accuracy & Mistakes: Mistakes penalize your score multiplier, meaning deliberate precision is often faster than reckless tapping.
  • Global Percentile Rank: Your final score is ranked against real human players globally. A finish of Top 1% indicates you outperformed 99% of players on today's grid.

The 100-Player Battle Royale Replay

Every time you finish a game, you can launch the Battle Royale Replay. This interactive canvas visualization puts you directly into a 100-player race, showing your real run competing neck-and-neck against 99 other players worldwide. Slower racers get eliminated in real-time waves, letting you watch your avatar battle for the podium and cross the finish line!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do I need to create an account or sign in?
No! Tiledly uses an anonymous, persistent device identifier stored in your browser. Your streak and stats are tracked automatically without any passwords or email sign-ins.
What time does the daily challenge reset?
The challenge resets every day at 00:00 UTC (Universal Time Coordinated). Everyone worldwide plays the exact same grid on the same calendar day.
Can I practice without affecting my daily streak?
Yes! Simply tap 🎯 Practice Mode on the home screen to play randomly seeded practice boards as many times as you like.
What is Tiledly PRO?
Tiledly PRO is an optional supporter pass that removes all banner ads, unlocks full calendar archive access to replay past puzzles, awards a VIP golden crown on the Battle Replay racer, and includes automatic streak protection if you miss a day.
