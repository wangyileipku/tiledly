Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026 • Effective Date: October 5, 2026

Welcome to Tiledly ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to respecting and protecting the privacy of everyone who plays our daily challenges at tiledly.com and associated domains.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data when you access or interact with our game.

1. Information We Collect

Tiledly is designed with privacy-first principles. We do not require personal account registration (no real names or passwords required to play):

  • Anonymous Device Identifier: A randomly generated UUID stored in your browser's local storage to save your daily streak, challenge history, personal bests, and anonymously submit your score to our global daily leaderboard.
  • Gameplay Records: Completion time, accuracy percentage, mistake count, and final score for daily challenge sessions.
  • Technical Log Data: Like most web applications, our servers automatically receive basic device telemetry, including browser type, operating system, and IP address for security and rate-limiting purposes.

2. Cookies, Local Storage & Third-Party Advertising

We use standard browser storage technologies to maintain game state:

  • Local Storage: Used to store your current streak, daily results, game audio preferences, and PRO membership status directly on your device.
Google AdSense & Third-Party Vendor Disclosure:

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting aboutads.info.

3. Payment Information & Tiledly PRO

If you choose to subscribe to or purchase Tiledly PRO, payment processing is handled exclusively by trusted third-party PCI-DSS compliant payment processors such as Stripe. Tiledly never collects, processes, or stores your credit card numbers or financial credentials on our servers.

4. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely for the following operational purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and calculate daily puzzle leaderboards and percentiles.
  • To synchronize daily streaks and prevent duplicate submissions.
  • To deliver non-intrusive advertising and verify PRO ad-free entitlements.
  • To detect and prevent client-side score manipulation or automated bots.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All local streak and game preferences are retained in your browser's local storage. You can delete this data at any time by clearing your browser cache and cookies for this site.

6. GDPR (EU/EEA) & CCPA (California) Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or California, you have specific statutory data privacy rights:

  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may accept or decline optional analytics and advertising cookies at any time using our Cookie Preferences banner. We implement Google Consent Mode v2, which defaults non-essential storage to denied until explicit user consent is given.
  • Right of Access & Deletion: Because Tiledly does not require registration, all gameplay history and personal bests are tied solely to your anonymous device ID in local storage. You can exercise your right to erasure at any time simply by clearing your browser cache and cookies.
  • Do Not Sell/Share My Personal Information: We do not sell your personal data. You can opt out of personalized third-party ad targeting via our cookie banner or through industry opt-out portals.

7. Children's Privacy

Tiledly is a general audience puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe personal information has been inadvertently provided, please contact us immediately.
