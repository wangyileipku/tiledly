How to Play Tiledly

Your complete guide to the daily speed challenge — rules, scoring, strategies, and more

What is Tiledly?

Tiledly is a fast-paced daily mental agility game played directly in your browser — no downloads or sign-ups required. Inspired by the daily ritual of games like Wordle and the adrenaline of reaction-speed tests, Tiledly serves up one shared challenge every day at midnight UTC. Every player worldwide sees the exact same seeded grid, so your time is a fair, direct comparison against thousands of global competitors.

The goal is simple: complete the puzzle as fast as possible with as few mistakes as you can manage. After finishing, you see exactly where you rank on the global leaderboard — and you can watch yourself race against 99 real players in the Battle Royale Replay.

The Basics

⚡ One challenge per day. The grid resets every day at 00:00 UTC. All players worldwide play the identical seeded board — your performance is directly comparable.
  • No account needed. Tiledly uses an anonymous persistent identifier stored in your browser. Your streak and personal bests are saved automatically.
  • One attempt per day. Once you complete the daily challenge, your score is locked in. Use Practice Mode to keep playing without affecting your ranking.
  • Global leaderboard. After completing the challenge, your percentile rank is calculated against all real human players who played that day.
  • Streak tracking. Play on consecutive days to build your streak. Miss a day and it resets — unless you have PRO streak protection.

Game Modes Explained

Tiledly cycles through 7 core game modes — one per day of the week — across a 4-week rotation matrix (28 distinct challenges in total). Each mode targets different cognitive skills: arithmetic, working memory, visual scanning, spatial reasoning, and more.

Monday
🧮
Sum Hunt
Find pairs or trios of tiles that add up to the displayed target sum. Tap all matching combinations as fast as possible before time pressure mounts.
💡 Tip: Scan for the highest and lowest numbers first — they're often part of the target pair.
Tuesday
🔢
Number Rush
Sprint through numbers 1 to 25 in ascending order (or 25 to 1 in countdown variants). Tests peripheral vision, visual scanning efficiency, and pure reaction speed.
💡 Tip: Don't chase the number — let your eyes scan the whole grid before each tap.
Wednesday
🎨
Color Cascade
Cycle through a repeating color sequence (Red → Orange → Yellow → Green → Blue) or navigate Stroop interference puzzles where the tile's text color conflicts with the word displayed.
💡 Tip: Focus on the ink color, not the word — your brain will try to read the word first.
Thursday
🧠
Memory Grid
You get 4 seconds to memorize all tile positions before they flip face-down to ?. Then tap each number in the correct ascending sequence from pure working memory.
💡 Tip: Group tiles into quadrants during the memorize phase — don't try to memorize them one by one.
Friday
💣
Minefield
Hidden bomb tiles are briefly revealed before the game starts — memorize them! Then clear all safe numbers in ascending order without detonating a single bomb.
💡 Tip: Trace bomb locations in a mental map while memorizing, not just individual positions.
Saturday
⚡
Math Blitz
Rapid mental arithmetic — PEMDAS equations, multiplication tables, percentages, and answer-bucket classification — all under intense time pressure. Solve fast, tap the correct answer tile.
💡 Tip: Round tricky percentages to the nearest 10 to get a fast approximate answer first.
Sunday
🔤
Alpha Hunt
Locate and tap letters A through Y in alphabetical sequence, or spell out a hidden 5-letter theme word by finding its letters across the grid in the correct order.
💡 Tip: Mentally split the alphabet into thirds (A–H, I–P, Q–Y) to narrow your search zone fast.

How Scoring Works

Your Tiledly score is built from three components measured with high precision:

Component How It's Measured Impact
Elapsed Time Millisecond precision using the browser's high-resolution performance.now() timer — starts on first tap, stops on final correct tile. Primary ranking factor. Lower is better.
Mistakes Every incorrect tap is counted. Mistakes add a time penalty to your effective score. Each mistake inflates your score penalty — sometimes it's faster to be accurate than rapid.
Accuracy Correct taps ÷ total taps, expressed as a percentage. Shown on the result screen and share card. Influences score multiplier.
Global Percentile Your effective score ranked against all verified human completions for the day's puzzle. Top 1% means you outperformed 99% of all players that day.

Scores are collected throughout the day and the leaderboard is updated in real time. Your percentile will shift as more players complete the challenge.

Battle Royale Replay

Once you finish the daily challenge, tap 🎬 Watch Battle Replay to launch the Battle Royale Replay visualizer. This animated canvas simulation lets you see how you race against other players across the world:

  • Your avatar moves in real time based on your actual completion pace.
  • Other racers are generated from real global player data sampled from that day's leaderboard distribution.
  • Elimination waves knock out slower players at regular intervals, narrowing the field to the final podium.
  • PRO members get a golden crown on their avatar, making their racer easy to spot in the crowd.

The Battle Royale is asynchronous — you're not playing live against others in real time, but the simulation is calibrated to real human performance data, so the race feels genuinely competitive.

Practice Mode

Tap 🎯 Practice Mode on the home screen at any time — before or after completing the daily challenge. Practice Mode generates an unlimited stream of randomly seeded boards using the current day's game mode rules.

  • Practice runs never affect your daily ranking or streak.
  • Great for warming up before the daily challenge or drilling a specific mode you find difficult.
  • Your personal best practice time is tracked separately from your daily score.
  • Available before and after completing the daily challenge.

Tips & Strategies

  • 👁️
    Scan the whole grid before your first tap. For Number Rush and Alpha Hunt, spending 0.5 seconds scanning for your first two targets saves far more time than diving in blindly and scanning tile-by-tile.
  • 🎯
    Accuracy beats raw speed. Each mistake adds a penalty to your effective score. On harder modes like Memory Grid and Minefield, a clean zero-mistake run will consistently outrank a faster but sloppy run.
  • 🧠
    Use spatial chunking for Memory Grid. During the 4-second memorize phase, mentally divide the grid into quadrants and assign 5–6 tiles to each zone rather than trying to memorize every position individually.
  • ⚡
    Build a daily routine. Playing at the same time each day (e.g., morning coffee) conditions your brain for peak performance and makes streak maintenance effortless.
  • 🎮
    Use Practice Mode to drill weak modes. If Math Blitz or Color Cascade consistently costs you rankings, spend 10 minutes in practice on those specific modes before the next occurrence.
  • 📱
    Install the app for faster load times. Add Tiledly to your home screen as a PWA for near-instant startup and a distraction-free full-screen experience — no browser bars eating into your peripheral vision.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time does the daily challenge reset?
The challenge resets every day at 00:00 UTC (Universal Coordinated Time). All players worldwide play the exact same grid on the same calendar day. Check your local UTC offset to know your exact reset time — for example, that's 8:00 PM Eastern US time in summer.
Do I need an account or to sign in?
No account is required. Tiledly uses an anonymous, persistent device identifier stored in your browser's local storage. Your streak, stats, and daily results are tracked automatically with no password or email sign-in needed. Clearing your browser data will reset your local stats.
How is my streak tracked?
Your streak increments by 1 each calendar day you complete the daily challenge before the next reset at 00:00 UTC. Miss a single day and your streak resets to 0 — unless you have Tiledly PRO, which includes automatic streak protection for missed days.
What is Tiledly PRO and what does it include?
Tiledly PRO is an optional supporter pass (monthly or one-time lifetime). PRO members get: no banner ads, full Daily Archive access to replay every past puzzle, a golden crown VIP avatar in the Battle Royale Replay, and automatic streak protection so one missed day won't break your streak.
How do I share my result?
On the results screen, tap 📋 Share Result. Tiledly generates a spoiler-free share card showing your time, accuracy, percentile, and streak — no grid solution is revealed. You can copy it to clipboard and paste it anywhere, or use native sharing on mobile.
Can I replay past challenges from the archive?
Yes! Tap 📅 Daily Archive on the home screen to browse every past daily challenge. Free users can view the archive list and see past scores. PRO members can replay any past puzzle in full and submit archive scores.
Is a new mode released every day?
Yes — a new challenge unlocks every day at midnight UTC. The game cycles through 7 core modes (one per day of the week) across a 4-week rotation, creating 28 unique variations before repeating. So you'll see Sum Hunt every Monday, but with a different seeded grid and target each week.
Which browsers are supported?
Tiledly works in all modern browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and any Chromium-based browser. It runs best on up-to-date versions. For the optimal experience on mobile, add Tiledly to your home screen as a PWA — this gives you a full-screen distraction-free mode with faster load times.
